# Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and safe learning community.

Contributors and participants should:

- Treat others with respect.
- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Protect student privacy.
- Avoid discriminatory, harassing, or humiliating behavior.
- Credit the work of others.
- Prioritize safety in engineering activities.

Serious concerns may be reported privately to the repository maintainers.
